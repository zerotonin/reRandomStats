Passive Voice:

Statistical analyses were conducted using Python 3.7 and its libraries numpy, pandas, and tqdm. A non-parametric test, 
specifically the Ronald Fisher permutation test on median differences, was employed to determine statistical significance 
for cell counts. The median was chosen as the statistic of interest given that the data consists of integer counts and no 
assumptions were made about the underlying distribution. P-values are reported in the corresponding figure legends. When 
multiple comparisons were made, the p-values were corrected using the Benjamini-Hochberg false discovery rate algorithm 
(Benjamini & Hochberg, 1995; implementation provided by Seabold, Skipper, and Perktold, 2010 in statsmodels library).
